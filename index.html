About ImWeb
ImWeb

Real-time video synthesis instrument for the browser.

A reimagining of Image/ine by Tom Demeyer and Steina Vasulka (STEIM Amsterdam, 1997/2008). Not a port or a recreation — a new instrument in the same lineage.
