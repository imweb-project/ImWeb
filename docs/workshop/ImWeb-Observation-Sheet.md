# ImWeb — observation sheet

For the person running a workshop, or a student helping them. The aim is to
find out **where people get stuck**, not to test the students. If someone is
stuck, that is a fault in ImWeb, and it is exactly what this sheet is for.

How to use it: watch two or three students closely rather than the whole
room. Don't help straight away — count to ten first, and note what they try.
Then help, and note what fixed it.

## Five moments to watch

For each one, tick how it went and write what you saw.

| # | Moment | Easy | Hesitated | Needed help | What did they try? What fixed it? |
|---|---|:-:|:-:|:-:|---|
| 1 | **The first screen.** Do they choose a way in, and do they understand what it offers? | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ | |
| 2 | **Changing a value.** Do they discover dragging sideways on their own? | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ | |
| 3 | **Finding a control.** Ask: "make the picture spin" or "add feedback". Do they find it — by browsing, or with ⌘K? | ☐ | ☐ | ☐ | |

## Five questions at the end

Ask the room, or a few students one at a time. Short answers are fine.

1. What did you make that you liked?
2. What was the most confusing moment?
3. What did you want to do but couldn't find?
4. Would you use it again on your own? For what?
5. If you could change one thing about it, what would it be?
